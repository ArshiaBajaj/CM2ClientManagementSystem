# Coach Me 2 — Data Retention and Deletion Schedule (draft for approval)

We keep personal information only as long as we need it to coach, keep everyone safe, and meet our obligations.

| Information | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Active students' profile, sessions, goals, journal, chat, forms | While enrolled | Archive when coaching ends |
| Archived students (left the program) | 2 years after their last session | Delete, unless a safety concern is open |
| Parents' accounts and contact details | Same as their teen | Delete with the teen's record |
| Safety concerns and related notes | 7 years (or longer if legally required) | Review, then delete |
| Signed agreements and consents | 3 years after the person leaves | Delete |
| Intern accounts, tasks, commitments, team chat | End of the internship year + 1 year | Delete |
| Coach accounts and onboarding forms | 3 years after they stop coaching | Delete |
| Audit log (sign-ins, views, changes) | 2 years | Delete |
| Welcome links | 14 days, single use | Expire automatically |
| Spreadsheet import files | Not stored by the portal | Delete your downloaded copy after importing |

**Requests from families.** A parent (or a student who is 18+) can ask to see, correct, or delete their information.
The director confirms who is asking, answers within 30 days, and keeps a note of the request. Safety records may be
kept if needed to protect a child or meet a legal duty.

**Who does this.** The technical lead runs the yearly clean-up each August and reports to the director.
