# Warm Meridian

A movement about the distance between where someone stands and where they want to go, drawn with the patience of a cartographer and the warmth of a handwritten letter.

**Space and form.** Composition begins with quiet: generous margins, a single horizon, and long meridian lines that curve like the edge of a globe. Forms are few and exact: a point, an arc, a ring. They sit on a strict grid that never announces itself. Every curve is drawn and redrawn until it rests at precisely the right tension, the result of painstaking attention rather than a default.

**Color and material.** The ground is warm paper, never clinical white, with an almost imperceptible grain that keeps the surface human. Ink is a deep blue-black. One clear blue carries meaning, a single leaf green marks arrival, and a small ember of clay appears only where a moment matters most. Color is rationed like gold leaf, so each use feels meticulously decided.

**Scale and rhythm.** Repetition builds trust: rings of equal step, meridians at measured intervals, tick marks counted out one by one. Against this patient rhythm, one gesture is allowed to be large, a path traced from one point to another. The contrast between the dense, careful field and the single human gesture is the heart of the work, and it must read as master-level execution.

**Typography.** Letters are a voice, not a label maker. A warm, slightly literary serif speaks the few words that matter, often in italic, as if spoken aloud. A quiet grotesk and a small monospace handle coordinates and annotations, set tiny and tracked wide. Text stays sparse: a phrase, never a paragraph. Each word is placed with the care of someone who has spent a career on the space between letters.

**Hierarchy.** The eye travels the way a person does: from a starting point, through a field of possibilities, to a destination marked with calm certainty. Nothing shouts. Nothing is ornamental. The finished piece should look like the product of countless hours of refinement by a deeply expert hand: restrained, exact, and unmistakably made by a person.
